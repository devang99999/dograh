SECTION 1 — WHAT IS ANTOC AI CRM?
================================================================================

Antoc AI CRM is a real-estate-specific CRM built in Ahmedabad, India. It is
designed for real estate brokers, independent agents, developers, builder teams,
and channel partners. It consolidates leads from portals, Meta, and Google into
one dashboard, automates WhatsApp follow-ups, deploys an AI voice calling agent
that qualifies leads within seconds of inquiry, and tracks every lead from first
contact through deal closure.

It is NOT a general-purpose CRM. It is built only for real estate — which means
every field, every pipeline stage, every automation rule is designed for how
real estate sales actually works in India.

Headquarters: Ahmedabad, India.
Target customers: Brokers (solo and teams), developers, builder sales teams,
  channel partners (CPs), real estate co-working operators, property consultants.
Language support in AI agent: Hindi, Hinglish, Gujarati, English.
Data storage: India-only servers, compliant with Indian data laws.

Deduplication: if the same phone number comes from two portals, Antoc merges
into one lead profile automatically.

2.2 AI VOICE CALLING AGENT — HOW IT WORKS
-------------------------------------------
When a new lead arrives (from any source), Antoc's AI agent calls the lead
automatically. Default delay: 30 seconds after lead creation. Configurable
to immediate or up to 24 hours.

The AI agent call flow:
  Step 1 — Greets lead by name ("Namaste Rahul ji, main Neha bol rahi hoon…")
  Step 2 — Confirms the inquiry: project name, location
  Step 3 — Qualifies: budget, timeline, intent (self-use vs investment),
           BHK preference, family size
  Step 4 — Answers basic project questions (reads from project KB)
  Step 5 — Hot lead → connects to live agent or books site visit directly
            Warm lead → schedules callback, sends WhatsApp with brochure
            Cold / wrong number → marks disposition and logs

After every call:
  - Full call recording saved to lead profile
  - Transcript (text) saved
  - AI-generated summary: "Lead interested in 3BHK, budget 80L, site visit
    preferred Saturday morning"
  - Disposition tag: hot / warm / cold / not_interested / callback / wrong_number

Human agent receives the lead with full context before they speak a single word.

Retry logic: if the lead does not answer, the system retries up to 3 times over
24 hours. After 3 unanswered calls, it sends a WhatsApp message instead.

2.3 WHATSAPP AUTOMATION — FULL DETAIL
---------------------------------------
Antoc sends WhatsApp messages automatically based on triggers and timing rules.
All messages go through the official WhatsApp Business API (Meta BSP — no
grey-route, no ban risk).

Trigger-based automations (fire once when event happens):
  - New lead created → welcome message + brochure PDF
  - Site visit booked → confirmation + Google Maps link
  - Site visit tomorrow → reminder
  - Deal won → congratulations + next steps
  - Payment due → reminder
  - Lead goes cold (no activity 7 days) → re-engagement message

Time-based drip sequences:
  - Day 0: Welcome + brochure
  - Day 2: Project highlight (video or image)
  - Day 5: Testimonial or case study
  - Day 10: Limited availability / offer
  - Day 21: "Aapko koi baat karni hai toh main available hoon"

Bulk broadcast:
  - Festival messages (Diwali, New Year, Navratri) to your full lead base
  - New project launch announcement
  - Price revision notice
  - Offer / scheme announcement

Messages are template-based. Templates are pre-approved by Meta. You can
customise text, attach images, PDFs, and videos. No per-message cost above the
BSP fee. Unsubscribe handling is automatic and compliant.

2.4 LEAD PIPELINE — STAGES AND CUSTOMISATION
----------------------------------------------
Visual Kanban board. Every lead is a card. Drag to move between stages.

Default pipeline stages:
  New Lead → First Contact → Interested → Proposal Sent →
  Site Visit Scheduled → Site Visit Done → Negotiation →
  Deal Won → Deal Lost → On Hold

You can rename stages, add stages, reorder, or remove. Each stage can have:
  - Automatic WhatsApp message on entry
  - Automatic task assignment ("Call within 2 hours")
  - Colour coding
  - SLA timer (if lead stays in stage >N hours, alert manager)

AI agent updates the stage automatically based on disposition. Human agents
can override anytime.

Filter and search the pipeline by:
  - Source (99acres, Meta, etc.)
  - Agent assigned
  - Project name
  - Stage
  - Date range
  - Budget range
  - Lead score (AI-assigned)

2.5 LEAD SCORING
-----------------
Antoc's AI scores every lead 1–100 based on:
  - Response speed (answered AI call vs ignored)
  - Budget match (is budget realistic for the project?)
  - Intent signals (site visit request = high score)
  - Engagement (opened WhatsApp messages, replied)
  - Source quality (referral > portal > bulk campaign)

Hot leads (score 70+) trigger an immediate notification to the sales manager.
Agents can sort their pipeline by score to prioritise.

2.6 TEAM & AGENT MANAGEMENT
-----------------------------
  - Unlimited agent accounts on Growth and Enterprise plans

2.7 CHANNEL PARTNER (CP) MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------

  - CP commission calculation: set % or flat fee per project

  - CP performance leaderboard: which CPs are active, which are dormant
  - Automated brokerage payable report for accounts

2.8 PROJECT & INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------

  - Add floors, towers, unit types

    Green = available, Yellow = reserved/on-hold, Red = sold
  - Hold a unit against a lead (soft hold, configurable duration)
  - Price escalation: update all unit prices in bulk by % or fixed amount

SECTION 3 — PRICING (POSITIONING ONLY — NEVER QUOTE INR ON CALL)
================================================================================

Pricing depends on: team size, number of projects, and whether AI calling is
activated. There are three tiers:

Starter: Solo broker or small team (2–4 people). Core CRM, portal integration,
  WhatsApp basics. No AI calling.

Growth: Teams of 5–25. Full WhatsApp automation, AI calling agent, lead scoring,
  CP management, mobile app, reports. This is the most popular plan.

Enterprise: Large developers, multi-branch offices. Unlimited users, dedicated
  onboarding manager, SLA, white-label option, Tally integration, custom API.

Pricing philosophy:
  - Monthly billing available (no annual lock-in on monthly)
  - Annual plan: ~20% cheaper
  - Free trial: 14 days, all features, no credit card
  - ROI framing: one saved deal per month more than covers any plan cost. A
    single 2BHK deal in Ahmedabad is worth ₹30,000–₹1,50,000 in brokerage.
    Antoc prevents the follow-up misses that lose those deals.

IMPORTANT: Never say "yeh sasta hai" or quote ₹ numbers. Say "reasonable hai,
team size par depend karta hai. Demo ke baad pricing detail mein baat karte hain."

SECTION 7 — CUSTOMER PERSONAS AND WHAT THEY CARE ABOUT
================================================================================

BROKER — INDIVIDUAL (solo agent, 1–5 years experience)
  Pain: Too many leads to track manually. Misses follow-ups. Uses WhatsApp
    and Excel. Loses warm leads because he called too late or forgot.
  What to emphasise: AI calling agent (calls lead before he can), WhatsApp
    automation (never forget a follow-up), mobile app (manage on phone).
  Fear: Too expensive for one person, too complicated to learn.
  Reassurance: Starter plan, 15-min mobile app learning curve, free trial.

BROKER — TEAM (5–20 agents, established agency)
  Pain: Leads from multiple portals in multiple tabs. No visibility on which
    agent is following up. Duplicate calls to same lead. No reports.
  What to emphasise: Portal aggregation, team dashboard, lead assignment,
    agent performance reports, CP management.
  Fear: Team won't adopt it, data migration complexity.
  Reassurance: Managed onboarding (3–4 days), data migration handled,
    mobile app simple, manager can track adoption.

DEVELOPER / BUILDER SALES TEAM
  Pain: Multiple projects, multiple agents, inventory management nightmare,
    channel partner tracking, post-sales handover.
  What to emphasise: Inventory grid, project microsite, CP management,
    payment schedule, site visit workflow.
  Fear: Integration with their existing ERP / tally, data security.
  Reassurance: Tally integration (beta), robust data security, API access,
    dedicated Enterprise onboarding.

CHANNEL PARTNER (CP)
  Pain: Developer shares leads but no tracking, commission disputes, no
    visibility on deal status.
  What to emphasise: CP portal (own login, own lead view), commission
    tracking, deal attribution transparency.
  Fear: Developer may not use it.
  Response: "CP portal is also convincing for the developer to join — they
    can track your performance too."
